12 distinct subjects across the 412 reports · intake's tag disagrees with the
tenant's cause 38 times.

- parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, so a day-first tenant’s dates
come out with day and month swapped. This is the bug the ticket was opened for.

- hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it tells
them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. A different bug in a different
place; the desk’s own table already marks it “not this ticket”.

- oneDayMove: the date moves by one day, not by months: a Tuesday shows as Monday, one
month is a day off, a client in Lisbon and one in Toronto see different days. That is a
timezone or date-boundary bug. The desk noticed these and footnoted them under parser.

Why the ticket hid them

- Merged by subject. Decision 0001 </kb/decisions/0001-dedupe-by-subject> merges reports
whose subject lines match and nobody reads the body. “Wrong day on the invite” fits all
three problems, so all three landed in one ticket.

- Tagged by browser. Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser language; the
cause depends on the tenant and the form they run. Counting tags shows how often the two
disagree, not what broke.

- Footnoted, not split. The one-day moves were seen, then counted under the tenant’s cause.
A footnote does not reach engineering.

- Counted twice. The export comes in overlapping pages; the table counts each id once.

What each ticket is for

- parser: fix how the shared parser reads dates for day-first tenants. When it ships, parser reports should stop; hint and oneDayMove will not.

- hint: make booking-v3’s hint and its reading agree with the tenant’s locale.

- oneDayMove: find where a date crosses a day boundary (time zone, UTC midnight,
daylight saving) and keep the booked day.

Each problem links to its ticket in the table. Decision 0001 stands as written; replacing it is
a decision record of its own.

Lessons

- A big ticket can be several bugs that share a subject line. Count by cause, not by subject.

- Read a sample of bodies before calling a ticket one issue.

- A footnote is a finding nobody acts on. If it is a different problem, give it a ticket.

- An intake tag is a guess. The tenant decides the cause.
